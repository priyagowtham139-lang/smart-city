Our story

From flood response to a city that learns

In 2019 a monsoon flood showed the city its own blind spots: separate departments, separate data and no shared picture. The council formed Stackly to fix that.

Seven years on, roads, power, water, health and permits share one secure platform. Departments still own their services. Stackly provides the plumbing, the standards and the open data that make them work together.

Mission

Make every public service faster, fairer and easier to hold to account.

Vision

A net-zero city where technology quietly serves every neighbourhood equally.
